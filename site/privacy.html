Gamy Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026 · Contact: gamyd022@gmail.com

Gamy is a study app with a talking bear: it reads the learning modules you upload, makes quizzes and practice problems from them, and answers questions about them. This policy explains what Gamy keeps, what leaves your device, and your choices. Gamy is for people aged 18 and older.

1. The short version

  • Your lessons, quizzes, scores and chats are kept on your device.
  • Gamy's AI runs on your device. Your modules are never sent to an outside AI service.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. We never sell or share your personal information.
  • Only if you choose to sign in with Google, a copy of your subject names and module text is kept online so you can restore it.

2. What stays on your device

Subjects, modules (the text read from your files or photos), quizzes, scores, chats, settings, and the optional AI models. Photos of modules are read on your device; only the text found in them is kept. Deleting a module, a subject or the app removes them, and Settings → Delete all my data removes everything at once.

4. How long we keep it

  • Online lesson copy: until you delete it. Settings → Account → Delete account permanently erases the online copy and your account at once. Signing out keeps your lessons on the device.
  • Crash reports: kept by Sentry for a limited period (up to 90 days) and then deleted.
  • Report emails: kept only as long as needed to handle the report.

5. Security

Your online copy is stored in your own private folder: security rules allow only your signed-in account to read or change it. Connections use encryption (HTTPS).

6. Your rights

We process personal information in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) of the Philippines. You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information by emailing gamyd022@gmail.com. You can also delete your online copy yourself at any time with Delete account.

7. Other services' policies

These services have their own privacy policies: Google (Sign-In, Firebase, Google Play), Microsoft (Microsoft Store), Wikimedia (Wikipedia), Hugging Face and Sentry. The EmbeddingGemma model is provided under the Gemma Terms of Use; the Qwen3 model under the Apache 2.0 license.

8. Age

Gamy is intended only for people aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe a minor has used Gamy, contact us and we will delete their online copy.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, Gamy will ask you to review and accept the updated terms before you continue.
